5. What the research found so far

  • Tested 540 indicator combinations on 1h, 4h and 1d candles, chosen on older years and checked on years they were not chosen on, after Kraken fees.
  • Daily trend following (only buy in an uptrend, sell when the shorter trend breaks) cut the worst drop from 84% to about 52% but made about a quarter of buy-and-hold's return (2016-now).
  • Hourly and 4-hour strategies did not survive the fees. Finding a better strategy is parked for later.
